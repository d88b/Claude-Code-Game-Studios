## Long Description (长描述)

### Steam 版本 (英文)

```
ABOUT THIS GAME

Rust Magic Tide is a 2D side-scrolling pixel post-apocalyptic survival-building game where you drive a magitech battle wagon to scavenge the surface, dig and build an underground fortress, and defend against periodic zombie tides.

CORE FEATURES

🚗 MAGITECH BATTLE WAGON
- Customize your vehicle with weapons, armor, and magic upgrades
- Explore the dangerous surface world for valuable resources
- Manage your magic energy pool — deplete it and you're stranded
- Strategic retreat: know when to fight and when to flee

⛏️ UNDERGROUND FORTRESS BUILDING
- Dig deeper into the earth to expand your bunker
- Place defensive structures: turrets, barriers, workshops
- Build production facilities to process resources
- Design your layout for optimal zombie defense

🧟 ZOMBIE TIDE DEFENSE
- Survive periodic waves of increasingly powerful enemies
- Face different zombie types: fast runners, heavy tanks, ranged spitters
- Boss encounters challenge your defensive preparations
- Day/night cycle affects enemy aggression and danger level

🌍 EXPLORE & SURVIVE
- Discover new areas with unique resources and threats
- Balance exploration time with resource gathering
- Return safely to your bunker before night falls
- Progress through days, unlocking new technologies

THE LOOP

Deploy from your bunker → Explore the surface → Dig for resources → Battle zombies → Collect loot → Return before dark → Upgrade your fortress → Survive the next tide → Repeat

WHO IS THIS GAME FOR?

Rust Magic Tide is designed for players who enjoy:
- Strategic resource management (Terraria, RimWorld)
- Tower defense mechanics (They Are Billions)
- Vehicle customization (Metal Max series)
- Pixel art aesthetics and survival gameplay
- Mastery-focused progression systems

KEY DIFFERENCES

Unlike pure tower defense games, you actively explore and scavenge. Unlike pure survival games, you have a mobile battle wagon as your primary tool. The combination creates a unique rhythm: prepare, deploy, scavenge, retreat, defend.

CURRENT STATE

This is an early development build. Core systems are functional:
- Vehicle movement and combat
- Digging and building mechanics  
- Day/night cycle with danger scaling
- Basic enemy AI and wave spawning
- Save/load system with 3 slots

More features are planned for future updates.
```
